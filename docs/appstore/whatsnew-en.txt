Fern Pro is a subscription now: you set it up inside the app and access opens right after payment. Anyone who bought Pro earlier keeps it for good — nothing to do.

New:
• Fern account: sign in with email, Google or Apple. The subscription belongs to you, not to a phone: change your device and Pro comes with you.
• Pro works offline: the app keeps a signed expiry date and does not ask the server on every launch.
• Cancel with one tap: Settings → Fern Pro. Access stays until the end of the paid period.

Changed:
• The bottom bar became a dock: sections keep the row, and reviews moved to their own button — it shows how many cards are waiting and starts them from any section.
• Settings are laid out as blocks: every item with its own round icon, sections told apart by tone.
